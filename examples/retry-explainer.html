Read the model

Let p be failure probability and r the retry budget.

  • Maximum attempts: N = r + 1.
  • Chance of success: 1 − pᴺ.
  • Expected attempts: 1 + p + … + pʳ.
  • Delay before retry i, starting at 1: min(250 × 2ⁱ⁻¹, 4000) ms.

Know the limits

Failures are independent and have the same probability on every attempt. The model assumes unlimited server capacity and no change in failure probability during backoff.

What a real service can change

A shared outage makes failures correlated. Extra retries can overload a server. A lost response can hide a completed write. Use measured data, appropriate retry rules, and idempotency before applying a policy.

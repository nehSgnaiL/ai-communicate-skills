# AI Communicate Skills

**Help AI explain its work so you can understand it and decide what to do next.**

The skill is called **Communicate Clearly**. It helps AI choose a useful explanation: a few plain sentences, a picture, a page you can explore, or a short video when the tools are available.

## What can I ask for?

- “Explain what changed and why it matters.”
- “Draw how the parts work together.”
- “Let me try the options and see what changes.”
- “Show me the process step by step.”

The goal is to make the explanation easy to follow, with examples and reasons you can check.

## How do I start?

Ask your AI:

> Read https://github.com/nehSgnaiL/ai-communicate-skills/blob/main/README_AI.md and install Communicate Clearly for this project.

After it is installed, try:

> Use Communicate Clearly to explain this work. Start with the result, give me a concrete example, and tell me what is still uncertain.

To check for updates later, ask: “Check whether Communicate Clearly has an update, and show me what changed.”
